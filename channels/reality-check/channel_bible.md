## 1. Idea in one line
Asli jawab, asli research ke saath: duniya ke bade sawaal (science first, later other topics) samjhaye hue aasaan
Hinglish me, 18-20 min ki detail wali videos. Face nahi; voice + visuals.

## 2. What we do NOT do
- NOT "Movie vs Reality". Movies are only a research signal: which idea/question made people curious (time, space, DNA,
  AI, black holes...). The video topic is the real science question; a movie may be named at most as a hook, never a clip,
  poster, dialogue or music (shared RULES copyright / 12a).
- No politics or hot controversies at the start. No unsourced claims.
- No copied script/structure from other channels (study only the method, RULES 12c).

## 3. Topic research method
1. Khoji scout: which science ideas people love (movies, search demand), Hindi vs English saturation check, score, top 5.
2. Pick one -> Khoji dossier: every fact `[F#]` with 2+ reliable sources (primary first: NASA, ESA, CERN, papers,
   university pages, textbooks; Wikipedia only as a pointer). Verdict per claim: true / partly true / not true / unknown.
3. Title uses the real search word of the concept (not the movie).

## 4. Video format
- Long 18-20 min, chapters, a new question or reveal every ~2-3 min, hook in the first 30 s (first 1 s visual hook).
- Visuals (free first): own diagrams/animations/motion graphics, NASA/ESA public-domain footage, Wikimedia, simulations.
  AI images only if free option fails and the budget allows. Parallax alone is not enough for science.
- Every Long: 2-3 Shorts. Sources listed in each video description (promise on the channel page).
