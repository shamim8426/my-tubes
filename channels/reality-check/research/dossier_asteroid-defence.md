## 0. One-line summary
On 26 Sept 2022 NASA's DART spacecraft crashed into Dimorphos (moon of asteroid Didymos) and shortened its 11 h 55 min orbit by about 33 minutes - the first time humans changed the motion of a natural body. It worked far better than a simple "billiard ball" estimate (~7 min) because the blasted-out rock (ejecta) pushed back too. Europe's Hera probe is now arriving at the crash site (Nov 2026) to measure what really happened. No known asteroid threatens Earth in the next 100 years per NASA's checks of the objects it has found - but we have found only a fraction of the mid-size ones, which is why detection missions matter.
