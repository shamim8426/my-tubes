## E. Policy facts (verified 2026-11-08)
- support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851 (fetched): ad revenue share needs 1,000 subs AND (4,000 public watch hours in 12 months OR
  10M valid public Shorts views in 90 days). Shorts-feed watch hours do not count towards the 4,000.
- Early-access tier (secondary source citing YouTube creators page, search result 2026): 500 subs + 3 public uploads in 90 days +
  (3,000 watch hours/365 d OR 3M Shorts views/90 d) -> fan funding (memberships, Super Chat, etc.), NOT ad share. Verify in Studio.
- Secondary blogs mention a possible Feb 2027 change to 20M Shorts views for the Shorts path: NOT VERIFIED; check Studio Earn tab.
- "Reused/mass-produced content" and AI-voice policy: we declare synthetic media; narration must add original script/animation (we do).
