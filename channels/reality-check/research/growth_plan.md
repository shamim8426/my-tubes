## 0. Verdict: is our approach right?
**Short answer: the direction (faceless, calm Hinglish documentary with real public-domain footage + own animation) is sound, but "space/science first" as a narrow niche and 18-20 min as the default length are not the safest way to earn. Recommendation: BLEND.** Keep the look, voice and fact discipline; widen the TOPIC to "big real events and big questions explained with science" (space-mission disasters, famous accidents, black holes/aliens/time, deep sea, earth), start with 10-14 min Longs, move to 15-20 only when retention proves it.

Why (evidence):
- Demand is real: Hindi long science videos reach 13-25M views, but all such results came from one face-brand creator (Dhruv Rathee); the other Hindi 20M+ results are Shorts. English top space/black-hole videos reach 30-150M. So Hindi viewers want it; a quality faceless Hindi long-form supplier looks thin (judgement).
- But small faceless Hindi science channels (7K-50K subs after 6-12 months, unverified trackers) show it is not an easy lane. Pure abstract science (physics, time) has weak click-through and retention for a new channel with no brand. Events with a story (a disaster, a mission, a mystery) carry both a curiosity gap and built-in search demand, and science is the explanation layer. That is where our "asli jawab" brand still fits.
- Money: RPM estimates conflict 4-5x between blogs. Planning number (EST): INR 40-100 per 1,000 long-video views for Hindi education/science, Shorts INR 5-30. So 100K long views/month is about INR 4,000-10,000/month (EST). AdSense alone will be small for a long time.

Verdict by niche: stay in science-explained, but pick story-shaped topics. Do not pivot to money/geopolitics: higher RPM but crowded, footage weak, rules conflict, fact-check cost higher.

**Probability (honest, not 99%).** Nobody can promise it; YouTube outcomes are heavy-tailed. My rough estimate (EST, judgement, not data), assuming we upload consistently for 12 months (about 45-50 Longs, quality at least as good as video 1, fact-clean, titles tested):
- ~70-80% reach 1,000 subs; ~45-60% reach YPP ad-share (1,000 subs + 4,000 watch hours) within 12 months; ~25-35% that at least one Long passes 100K views; ~10% a breakout (1M+).
- Below 30 videos, or quitting after 3 months, drops these sharply; it is mostly a numbers-and-iteration game.
What drives it (in order): (1) CTR of title+thumbnail on the first 3-5 videos, (2) retention in the first 30 s and a flat curve afterwards, (3) a topic with real search/browse demand, (4) consistency (cadence kept for 6+ months), (5) no strike (copyright, misinformation, reused content). We control all five; luck/algorithm is the rest. Any claim of "99% sure" would be false.

## 1. YPP (verified at support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851 on 2026-11-08)
- Ad revenue: 1,000 subs AND (4,000 public long-form watch hours in last 12 months OR 10M valid public Shorts views in 90 days). Shorts-feed watch time does not count towards the 4,000 hours.
- Early access (fan funding only, not ads; secondary source, check Studio): 500 subs + 3 public uploads in 90 days + (3,000 hours/365 d OR 3M Shorts views/90 d). A rumoured Feb 2027 rise of the Shorts path to 20M is NOT VERIFIED.
- No fast-track for ads beyond the above. The Shorts route (10M views) is unrealistic and low-RPM; use Shorts only to feed Longs.
- Math (EST): 4,000 h = 240,000 min. At 7-8 min average view duration (15 min video, ~50% retention) = 30-35K long views; at 5 min = 48K views. Only public videos count (video 1 is still private: publish it when it passes review).
- Timeline (EST): 8-14 months at 1 Long/week; 5-7 months only if one or two videos break out (50K+ views); 18+ months if videos stay at 1-3K views. Plan for 12 months, treat earlier as a bonus.

## 2. Cadence we can sustain
- 1 Long per week (4-6 h machine each) + 2-3 Shorts cut from it (reuse visuals, max 40 s). Fixed day/time so the audience learns it. Do not promise more; consistency beats bursts.
- Batch research: one Khoji dossier + script per week; keep a bank of 6 approved topics so a bad week does not break the schedule.
- If tokens are tight: 1 Long per 10 days is acceptable, never two weeks of silence in the first 6 months.

## 3. Long vs Shorts
- Longs carry the money (RPM 5-10x Shorts, EST). Shorts = discovery only: ask the hook question, end with "poora jawab Long me", set Related video in Studio each time (already in our checklist).
- Length: start 10-14 min (higher retention % per video, easier to hold, still allows mid-roll ads from 8 min). Move to 15-20 only for topics that need it. 18-20 min as default is a risk for a channel with no returning audience (judgement).

## 4. CTR / retention principles for Hindi documentary
- Title (Hinglish, 45-60 chars): concept search word + curiosity/stakes, e.g. "Black Hole me gir gaye to kya hoga? | Reality Check - Asli". One clear promise the video really keeps (clickbait kills retention).
- Thumbnail: one big visual (real NASA image or clean render), 3-4 big words, high contrast, one focal point, readable at ~120 px; no face needed. Use Studio "Test and compare" if available.
- First 30 s: second 1 = visual hook; state the question + the surprising fact + why it matters to the viewer; promise the payoff; no logo/jingle intro, no subscribe plea before ~60 s.
- Retention: new question or reveal every 60-120 s (tighter than the bible's 2-3 min), visual change every 5-8 s, Hindi subtitles, calm pacing without dead air, end with a loop to the next video (end screen last 20 s).
- Language: plain Hindi (rule), each term explained once, Indian size comparisons (Delhi-Mumbai, cricket ground).
- Series/playlists: "Antariksh ke Haadse", "Brahmand ke Bade Sawaal", "Samundar ke Neeche"; max 3 series in the first 20 videos.
- Publish time (EST, general Indian-audience practice; verify with Studio "When your viewers are on YouTube"): weekdays 18:00-21:00 IST, weekends 10:00-12:00 or 18:00-20:00 IST. Our RULES 8 19:00 slot is consistent. Be public 1-2 h before the peak.

## 5. Using Analytics (48 h and 7 days; Nigraan job)
- Impressions under ~500 in 48 h: judge only after 3 videos (channel not yet trusted).
- CTR: under ~3% = fix title/thumbnail first (re-title within 48 h); 4-8% good (EST; Studio shows a channel benchmark).
- Average view duration + retention graph: locate the 0-30 s drop (hook), the cliff (boring chapter), spikes (repeat what works). Next video from: top "YouTube search" terms, "Suggested videos" sources, and the topics with the highest retention.
- Rule: CTR >= 4% AND retention >= 40% in the first two weeks -> sequel/series; otherwise change packaging (title/thumbnail/hook), not format, for 2 videos.
