## Facts
[F1] She is Khawla (also Khuwayla) bint Tha'laba (Ansari); husband Aws ibn as-Samit. Her complaint led to 58:1-4. Sources: Ahmad 27319 (Arna'ut: weak chain, Ibn Ishaq mudallis); Abu Dawud 2214; Ibn Kathir on 58:1. Name variant (Khawla/Khuwayla; Abu Dawud 2219 Arabic says Jamila) = ikhtilaaf. Basic event pakka (sahih Aisha hadith).
[F2] Aws pronounced zihar ("you are to me like my mother's back"). Ibn Kathir (Ahmad); Ahmad 27319. pakka.
[F3] Her complaint in Ibn Kathir: he consumed her wealth, wore out her youth, she bore him many children, when she grew old he made zihar; "O Allah, I complain to You." Ibn Majah 2063 (sahih: Albani, Zubair Ali Za'i) supports old-age/complaint core.
[F4] Aisha's statement. Sahih chain Aisha<-Urwah<-Tamim ibn Salama<-A'mash. Wordings DIFFER: Ibn Majah 188 (sahih) in fetched summary: "praise to Allah whose hearing encompasses all voices", woman came while Aisha was in a corner of the house, "could not hear what she said"; Ibn Majah 2063 / Nasa'i 3490 / Ahmad 6/46: heard some, some hidden. Bukhari mu'allaq (Tawhid, 7385) per Ibn Kathir: heard part, not all. SAFE: "Aisha was in the room, heard part, part was hidden." Do not quote an exact line.
[F5] 58:1 revealed about her: Ibn Majah 188/2063, Bukhari ta'liq, Ibn Kathir. pakka.
[F6] Expiation order (58:3-4): free a slave -> fast two consecutive months -> feed 60 poor. Quran text; Ibn Kathir 58:4; Abu Dawud 2214. pakka.
[F7] Araq/dates IN KHAWLA'S CASE: Ibn Kathir (Ahmad) / Abu Dawud 2214: free slave (he has none) -> fast 2 months (too old) -> feed 60 (he has none) -> "we will help him with an araq of dates"; she offered another araq; he told her give it as charity on his behalf. Grade: Ahmad 27319 weak chain; Abu Dawud 2214 no Albani grade on page = vivaadit. Araq size: 15-16 sa' (2214), 60 sa' (note), 30 sa' (2215) = ikhtilaaf.
[F8] SEPARATE case: Salama ibn Sakhr al-Bayadi (Abu Dawud 2213 Albani hasan / Zubair Ali Za'i weak; Tirmidhi 1198; Ibn Majah 2062): also got dates (araq). So araq appears in BOTH; keep stories separate.
[F9] Prophet's first reply: Abu Dawud 2214: "fear Allah; he is your cousin". NOT VERIFIED in my fetched text: "I think you are forbidden to him" (appears in some Ahmad/Ibn Jarir versions; check Ibn Kathir full page). Vivaadit.
[F10] Children: Ibn Kathir only "bore him many children". Wikipedia's child name Ar-Rabia = single weak source, EXCLUDE.
[F11] Umar's respect: Qurtubi / Ibn Kathir web copies: Umar stopped and listened long; "she whose complaint Allah heard above seven heavens." No isnad or grade found -> hedge ("kaha jaata hai") or cut.
