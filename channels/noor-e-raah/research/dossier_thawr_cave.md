## One-line summary
On the Hijrat (Rabi al-Awwal, 1 AH, about Sept 622 CE) the Prophet ﷺ and Abu Bakr (RA) hid three nights in a cave of Mount Thawr; when Quraysh searchers stood so near that Abu Bakr saw their feet, the Prophet ﷺ calmed him: "What do you think of two whose third is Allah?"

## Time and place (certain vs plausible)
- Year: 1 AH, ~622 CE (Hijrat starts the Islamic calendar; Umar's decision, Bukhari 3934).
- Month: sira says Rabi al-Awwal (arrival at Quba usually 8 Rabi al-Awwal, Monday; Ibn Ishaq/Ibn Hisham). Departure date has IKHTILAAF (late Safar vs 1 Rabi al-Awwal). Say only "Rabi al-Awwal ka mahina, pehla Hijri saal, ~622 eesvi". No exact date for the cave nights.
- Place: Jabal Thawr, a few km south of Makkah (commonly ~4-5 km; sources vary — say "Makkah ke dakshin mein, kuchh meel door"). Rocky, steep hill, cave high up with a low narrow mouth (modern accounts; plausible). Use "paththreeli, khadi pahadi".
- Scene details: night movement, desert heat by day, rocky ground, camels (camels attested F5/F10). Searchers followed tracks up to the mountain (Ibn Hisham, Ibn Kathir). Climate, vegetation, cave interior = plausible reconstruction only; no precise cave size.
