## (c) Caveats
- No real search-volume data (Google Trends/Keyword tool not reachable; WebSearch returned nothing useful). Pool sizes
  are YouTube result estimates and mostly measure recitation videos, not "Hindi meaning" demand.
- Only 8/10 searches; Falaq, Nas, Kafirun, Quraish, Sharh, Nasr, Humazah ranked purely by estimate.
- Consistent with learnings.md: Al-Fatiha got 9 search impressions in 3 days (98.6% Home) -> search phrase FIRST in
  title ("Surah X ka Matlab Hindi Mein | ..."), meaning must start by 30-40 s (first ayat early), short videos
  (these surahs are 3-6 ayat -> aim 2-4 min) so retention is easier than Fatiha's 7:54 (AVD 33%).
- Early views will mostly come via Home/Browse, not search; judge search demand only after 7-28 days of impressions
  by traffic source (Nigraan data) and re-rank then.
