## 2. DO NOT USE (viral false or unsourced)
- Abu Bakr bitten by a snake in Ghar-e-Thawr (his tear heals it): no sahih source; the top Hindi video on this has 495k views (data) and rides on it. Weak/fabricated.
- Spider web + dove at the cave mouth as fact: rests on a Musnad Ahmad report (3251) graded weak/hasan-disputed; at most "kuch riwayaton me", better to drop.
- The old woman who threw garbage on the Prophet (and he nursed her) and the Jewish neighbour's garbage: no source in the six books; fabricated by popular preachers.
- Bilal's marriage tale (Bengali videos with 20M views): later dubious report; do not use.
- Umar and the Nile letter, Umar "Ya Sariya, al-jabal", Umar walking with the servant on the camel into Quds as exact details: weak/disputed chains (Ibn Abd al-Hakam, Tabari, Ibn Asakir).
- Umar's burying his daughter alive (pre-Islam), Umar's flour-carrying for the hungry mother with children (many versions), Abu Bakr feeding the blind woman (Umar finds it was Abu Bakr): chain missing/weak.
- Ka'b ibn Zuhayr's Burda poem and cloak, Sumayyah's martyrdom details: chain weak / disputed.
- Umm Ma'bad (row 28).
- Rule: every candidate must pass a "does it exist in Bukhari/Muslim/Tirmidhi/Abu Dawud/Ahmad with a gradable chain or in Ibn Ishaq + another independent source" test at dossier time.

   Dossier Qs: Bukhari 3653 (the foot/"ma zannuka bi ithnayn") and 3905-3906 exact text; Quran 9:40 tafseer; the 3 days, the guide Ibn Uraiqit and Amir ibn Fuhayra (sahih or sirah?); Suraqa's horse sinking (Bukhari) and the Kisra bracelets promise (sirah/hasan?); what is weak (spider, dove, snake, Umm Ma'bad) - list in "chhode gaye" with reasons; the date.
