## LAGU KARO (agli video) — max 1-2 at a time
### 2026-11-19 round (postmortem `analytics/postmortem_round_2026-11-19.md`) — these come FIRST
- [2026-11-19, PAKKI (1 video, 1,067 impr) — Title + thumbnail, HISTORY Longs] **Title ki shuruat insaani haalat se,
  simple shabdon me; naam `|` ke baad.** Pattern: "<sachchi haalat, 5-8 shabd> | <Naam> ki Sachchi Kahani | <Surah/source>".
  Haalat imaandaar ho (wahi jo video me hai), koi clickbait/jhooth nahi (RULES). **Thumbnail:** ek bada subject (faceless
  figure ya cheez, frame ki ~1/2 height), dark/saaf background jo subject se contrast kare, max 3-4 bade shabd EK taraf
  (figure ke upar kabhi nahi), translucent band nahi; 168 px chauda karke padh ke dekho.
  Saboot: 7fikMFw0OUQ (Khawla) 15 Nov ko 1,059 SUGGESTED impressions, CTR sirf 0.9% -> agle din test band (7 views);
  title "Khawla bint Thalaba ki Kahani | ..." (anjaan naam pehle), thumbnail brown-on-brown, chhota figure, text figure par.
  Tulna: kmYyRGdykjY Home CTR 4.6%. Naapna: agli history Long ka suggested/browse CTR >= 3%.
- [2026-11-19, HINT / kam data, 2 videos same pattern] **Hook ke baad seedha scene/takraav; background + naam ~20 s ke baad,
  scene me ghol kar.** "Ye bahut purani baat hai... naam tha X... pati ka naam Y" jaisi line hook ke turant baad mat rakho.
  Saboot: 7fikMFw0OUQ 0:06 line 2 (purani baat + naam) par 100% -> 59% (10 s), 41% @30 s, 29% @39 s phir ~24% flat (19 engaged);
  kmYyRGdykjY 14-19 s "पहले नाम..." par 100% -> 72%. Naapna: agli Long ka retention @10 s >= 70%, @30 s >= 50%.
- [2026-11-19, HINT / kam data] **Topic mix:** surah explainers = search (title "Surah X ka Matlab Hindi Mein" chal raha:
  3fj1U_Q4Ku8 45 search impr CTR 4.4%, 5EjERhAwqRI 51 search impr) + achhi AVD (42% / 40%); history/Quran-linked sachchi
  kahaniyan = suggested test (Khawla 1,059 impr). Dono rakho; history me packaging (upar) sudharo.
- **SHORTS — har agle Short me ek naya sudhaar (2026-11-19 se):** **0-1 s rule:** pehla frame = clip ki sabse mazboot
  tasveer (wide establishing shot nahi, black/fade-in/title card nahi), awaaz <= 0.3 s me shuru, aur pehli line = clip ka
  sabse thos, seedha pal ek saral vaakya me (jaise "घर में बस बच्चों का खाना था, फिर भी मेहमान को खिलाया"). Short ka
