# Ucharan (Arabic -> Devanagari) — Surah An-Nas 114 | Khoji, 2026-11-03

Format (RULES 1c): each jumla block gets one `UCHARAN` line = the SAME Arabic words in Devanagari, read plainly by the Ustaad (NOT tilawat), shown under the Arabic. Kalam finalises.

**Sources:** Arabic = Tanzil Uthmani (`sources.json` `ayat[].text`; byte-check on PC pending). Latin check = Tanzil `ara-quran-la` (`translit_tanzil`) and quran411 `ara-quran-la1` (`translit_q411`). Fact `T3`.

**Rules (as 003/004):** ق=क़ · ف=फ़ · غ=ग़ · خ=ख़ · ذ=ज़ · ث=स · ع = vowel only · article joins as pronounced · last vowel dropped at the jumla end (waqf).
**Points to check:** (1) lam of al- before ن/ر etc. is assimilated: birabbi + alnnasi = **बिरब्बिन नास**; sudoori alnnasi = **सुदूरिन नास**; wa alnnasi = **वन नास**. (2) 114:3 `إِلَٰهِ` has a dagger alif: इलाहिन नास (Q411 'Ilaahin'). (3) `ٱلْوَسْوَاسِ` = अल-वसवास joined after شَرِّ: **शर्रिल वसवासिल ख़न्नास**. (4) `ٱلَّذِى` = अल्लज़ी (ي after kasra, Tanzil 'Allathee'). (5) `يُوَسْوِسُ` = युवस्विसु -> at ayah end not applicable (inside the jumla). (6) ayah-end `ٱلنَّاسِ` -> नास in every jumla.
