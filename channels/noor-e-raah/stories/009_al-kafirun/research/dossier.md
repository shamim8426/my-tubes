## 5. Script-ready kahani (3:30-4:30 Hindi narration ke liye, sirf ye tathya)
1. Ye Quran ki 109vi surah "अल-काफ़िरून" hai, 6 chhoti ayat; naam pehli ayat ke shabd se [P1]. Isse "अल-मुक़श्क़िशा" (shirk se bari kar dene wali) bhi kaha gaya, ye naam Surah Ikhlas ke saath bhi aata hai [P4].
2. Zyadatar ulama ke mutabiq ye Makki surah hai; kuch (jaise Ibn al-Zubayr) se Madani ki raay bhi naql hai [P2]. Nuzool ki mash'hoor fehrist me ye 18vi surah hai, Al-Ma'un ke baad aur Al-Fil se pehle [P3].
3. Makka me Quraysh ke sardar chahte the ki ibadat me koi beech ka raasta nikle - "kuch tum hamari maano, kuch hum tumhari"; aisi pesh-kashein baar-baar hui. Ye surah us ummeed ka saaf aur aakhri jawab bankar aayi [N1].
4. Jawab ki shuruaat "क़ुल" (kaho) se - yaani faisla Allah ki taraf se; khitaab us waqt ke us khaas giroh se tha [K1, N2].
5. Ayat 2-5: "jinki tum ibadat karte ho, main nahi karta; jiski main karta hoon, tum nahi karte" - do baar. Mufassireen ne iski hikmatein batayi: zor dekar har ummeed khatam karna; "na aaj, na aage kabhi"; aur "kis ki" ibadat ke saath "kis tarah" ki ibadat me bhi farq [U2-U5, K2].
6. Aakhri ayat "लकुम दीनुकुम व लिया दीन": tumhare liye tumhara deen, mere liye mera - ibadat aur aqeede me saaf alagaav; ye "sab dharm ek" ka elaan nahi [U6, K3].
7. Ye alagaav ibadat me hai, bartaav me nahi: jo log ladte nahi unke saath bhalai aur insaaf ka hukm Quran (60:8) me hai; Nabi ﷺ ne ek Sahabiya ko apni mushrik maa ke saath achha rishta rakhne ko kaha (Bukhari 2620) [K4].
8. Nabi ﷺ Fajr ki do sunnat rak'aton me ye surah aur Surah Ikhlas padhte the (Sahih Muslim 726) [Z1]; Hajj me tawaf ke baad ki do rak'at me bhi (Sahih Muslim 1218) [Z2].
9. Ek sahabi ko sone se pehle ye surah padhne ko kaha: "ye shirk se bari hone ka elaan hai" (Abu Dawud 5055, Tirmidhi 3403) [Z3].
10. Aakhri saar (3-5 lines): surah tauheed par saaf rehna sikhati hai - ibadat me koi milawat nahi, aur logon ke saath naram, insaaf-bhara bartaav [P5, K5].
