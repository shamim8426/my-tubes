# Ucharan (Arabic -> Devanagari) — Surah Al-Kafirun 109 | Khoji, 2026-11-18

Format (RULES 1c + 2i/2m): har ayah ki ek `UCHARAN` line = wahi Arabic shabd Devanagari me, narrator saade tareeke se padhta hai (tilawat NAHI), screen par Arabic ke neeche. Kalam finalise kare. Tags [U1]-[U6] = ek ayah ek tag (109:1-6). Sab ayat chhoti hain, isliye jumla-split ki zaroorat nahi; agar purana jumla-format chahiye to neeche "jumla split" column hai.

**Sources:** Arabic = Tanzil Uthmani v1.1 bytes (dossier 1.1; sha256 PC par baaki). Latin check = Tanzil `ara-quran-la` (`translit_tanzil`) aur quran411 `ara-quran-la1` (`translit_q411`), dono 2026-11-18 padhe (fact T3).

**Rules (as 003-008):** ق=क़ · ف=फ़ · غ=ग़ · خ=ख़ · ذ/ز/ض=ज़ · ث/ص/س=स · ع = sirf vowel ('ayn/hamza vowelon ke beech अ) · article joins as pronounced · ayah ke ant ka aakhri vowel giraya (waqf; tanween bhi) · beech ki ayah ke vowels rakhe · ۟ (small rounded zero) wali alif nahi padhi jaati.

**Points to check:**
(1) 109:1 `قُلْ يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلْكَٰفِرُونَ`: Tanzil "Qul ya ayyuha alkafiroona", Q411 "Qul yaa-ai yuhal kaafiroon" -> **क़ुल या अय्युहल काफ़िरून** (`يَٰٓ` = "yā" dagger alif + madd; `أَيُّهَا` shadda -> अय्युहा, wasl ke saath "अय्युहल"; `ٱلْكَٰفِرُونَ` dagger alif = का, ف = फ़; aakhri "na" waqf me gira).
(2) 109:2 `لَآ أَعْبُدُ مَا تَعْبُدُونَ`: Tanzil "La aAAbudu ma taAAbudoona", Q411 "Laa a'budu ma t'abudoon" -> **ला अअबुदु मा तअबुदून** (ع sukun = vowel only, 006 "अअतैना" jaisa; beech ke "अअबुदु" ka "दु" rakha, aakhri "na" gira).
(3) 109:3 = 109:5 `وَلَآ أَنتُمْ عَٰبِدُونَ مَآ أَعْبُدُ`: Tanzil "Wala antum AAabidoona ma aAAbudu", Q411 "Wa laa antum 'aabidoona maa a'bud" -> **व ला अन्तुम आबिदूना मा अअबुद** (`عَٰ` dagger alif = आ; "आबिदूना" ka "ना" beech me rakha; aakhri damma gira: "अअबुद").
(4) 109:4 `وَلَآ أَنَا۠ عَابِدٌ مَّا عَبَدتُّمْ`: Tanzil "Wala ana AAabidun ma AAabadtum", Q411 "Wa laa ana 'abidum maa 'abattum" -> **व ला अना आबिदुम मा अबत्तुम** (`أَنَا۠` ki alif par ۟ = chhota "ana"; tanween `دٌ` + `مَّ` shadda-meem = "आबिदुम मा", 008 "हिजारतिम मिन" jaisa; `عَبَدتُّمْ` me د ki awaaz ت me mil jaati hai (ت par shadda) -> "अबत्तुम", Q411 jaisa; Tanzil Latin "AAabadtum" sirf akshar dikhata hai. Munshi/user: "अबत्तुम" (jaisa padha jaata hai) rakha; "अबद्तुम" NAHI).
(5) 109:6 `لَكُمْ دِينُكُمْ وَلِىَ دِينِ`: Tanzil "Lakum deenukum waliya deeni", Q411 "Lakum deenukum wa liya deen" -> **लकुम दीनुकुम व लिया दीन** (`وَلِىَ` = "wa liya", ya par fatha; short "ya" ko channel ki shaili ("तरा", "रब्बुका") ke hisaab se "या" likha; aakhri kasra gira: "दीन").
(6) 109:3 aur 109:5 Arabic me ek jaisi hain; ucharan bhi hu-ba-hu ek jaisa rakho.
