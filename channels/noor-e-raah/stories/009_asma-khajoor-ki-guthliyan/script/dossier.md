## 2. Age / period (only what is sourced)
- Died aged 100 in 73 AH => born about 27 years before Hijrat (sources: Dhahabi, Ibn Kathir, Tahdhib). So at Hijrat she was roughly
  27. Grade: **anumaan** from the sources' 100-year figure; do NOT say "27 saal ki thi" as a fact in the video. Say "jawan".
- Age gap with Aisha: Ibn Kathir/Ibn Asakir "10 years older"; Dhahabi "13 to 19 years" = vivaadit. Skip.
- When exactly the marriage happened: Ibn Sa'd says she married Zubayr in Makkah before Hijrat and joined Madinah (the hadith itself
  does not say). The hadith reports the hardship in Madinah. Describe as "Hijrat ke baad Madinah me" only, per the user's frame
  (hadith says "Zubayr married me" and the land was in Madinah). Exact time of land grant = pata nahi.

## 3. Disputed / weak: EXCLUDE from the video
- "Prophet ﷺ told Asma she would have two belts in Jannah in place of them" (Tabarani, Mu'jam al-Kabir) = weak/disputed chain. Exclude.
- Zubayr later divorced Asma at Abdullah's request / and took Urwa (Ibn Sa'd): reported, contested, sensitive to Zubayr. Exclude.
- Dramatised dialogue between Asma and Abu Bakr about money, her blind grandfather Abu Quhafa gold story (Ahmad), the Abu Jahl slap
  story (Ibn Hisham, weak chain): exclude.
- "Prophet ﷺ gave Zubayr the land as a reward" beyond the word "aqta'" (grant): do not add a reason.
- Exact farsakh in km: hadith says 2/3 farsakh. The widely quoted "about 2 miles / 3 km" is the translator's conversion (farsakh = 3
  miles by common scholarly estimate, so ~2 miles = ~3.2 km). Say "lagbhag 3 kilometre (andaza)" or just "do tihaai farsakh".
- "Zubayr had a hundred-slave wealth later" etc.: later period, not this story. Do not mix.

## 4. What is certain vs plausible reconstruction (for visuals)
Certain from text: a horse, a water-carrying camel, a leather bucket, dough, date-stones on a head, a land plot outside town, a road,
a camel kneeling, an Ansari neighbourhood.
Plausible (era-correct, not stated by the hadith): Madinah 1 AH-3 AH: mud-brick (kacchi eent / lataki) houses with palm-trunk and
palm-frond roofs, narrow dusty lanes, date-palm groves (nakhlistan) with irrigation channels, wells with a rope-and-bucket,
leather water skins, clay pots, a modest open market (suq) with mats and baskets of dates, hand millstone (rahaa). Mark as
"kalpana/reconstruction", never "the hadith says".
**RULE CONFLICT (flag to caller): channel RULES 2/2a forbid ANY Sahabi in ANY form (not from behind, not far, no silhouette).
Asma is a Sahabiyya, so no figure of her at all. The requested "women from behind/far" is not allowed for the main-channel
long/sample. Possible only under RULES 2c if it were a 3D Imaan-Short with a fictional character, and even then not named Asma.**
Safe visual language: date-stones in a basket, a horse with an empty stable, the empty path to the grove, a hand-mill, a leather
bucket on a well rope, a camel kneeling in the distance (animal only), sunrise over palms, mud-brick lane with no person.

## 5. Hindi pronunciation notes (Devanagari for TTS)

- farsakh = फ़रसख़ (ख़ with nuqta); "do tihaai farsakh" = दो तिहाई फ़रसख़.

- Dhat al-Nitaqayn = ज़ातुन्-निताक़ैन ; nitaq = निताक़ (kamarband).
