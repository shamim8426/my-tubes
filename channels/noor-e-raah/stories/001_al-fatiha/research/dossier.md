## 4. Visual notes (sirf jagah aur mahaul — koi insaan nahi)

Niyam yaad: kisi Nabi, Sahabi, farishte ki koi tasveer, parchhai, silhouette, peeth, roshni ka aakaar — kabhi nahi.
Bheed nahi, riwayat ka abhinay nahi. Koi AI-likha harf nahi (Arabic/Hindi hum font se lagayenge).

- **Parichay:** khula hua mushaf jaisa *bina likhai* ka kitaab-ka-pehla-panna mood (sirf kaagaz ki texture, sunehri
  hashiya, koi akshar nahi) — ya subah ki pehli roshni me pahadiyon ke beech khulti ghaati ("kholne wali").
- **Shan-e-nuzul (7vi sadi ka Makka):** sookhi kaali-bhoori chattani pahadiyan, beech me tang ghaati (wadi), retili
  zameen, kuchh khajoor ke ped; mitti/patthar ke neeche, chaukor, chapti chhat wale ghar, lakdi ke darwaze; raat me
  tel ke mitti ke diye (chhota diya, nangi lau); taaron bhara saaf aasmaan; subah se pehle neela-sa ujala. Pakki sadkein,
  bijli, gumbad, minar, aaj ki imaaratein — NAHI (us waqt nahi the). Ka'ba ko na dikhana behtar (7vi sadi ka sahi roop
  dikhane ka pakka khaaka hamare paas nahi — "pata nahi").
- **1:1–1:3 (rehmat):** baarish ke baad hari ho rahi zameen, subah ki dhoop, paani ka chashma.
- **1:2 ('alamin):** alag-alag jahan — samandar, pahad, raat ka aakaash-ganga, parinde ka jhund (door se), khet.
- **1:4 (badle ka din):** khaali, shaant vishaal maidan, dhalta sooraj ya sunsaan ufaq — darawna nahi, gambhir.
- **1:5:** akela diya andhere me — ek hi roshni ki taraf.
- **1:6–1:7:** registaan me ek seedha saaf raasta ufaq tak; uske dono taraf bhatakte, ret me mitte raaste (koi aadmi
  nahi); door ek pakka raasta roshni ki taraf.
- Rang: garam ret, gehra neela raat, sunehra subah; shaant mahaul. Koi studio/artist naam prompt me nahi.
