## 4. Khatre

- **Edition copyright (P1/P2):** Aaj ki chhapaiyon (Tafseer-e-Usmani wagairah) me jode gaye notes, nishaan [۱]–[۷] aur
  sampaadan naye ho sakte hain. India me sirf "sweat of the brow" se copyright nahi banta, lekin creative sampaadki
  jod (jaise para-baant, numbering) ko Supreme Court ne bachaaya (EBC v. Modak, 2008 —
  [SpicyIP](https://spicyip.com/2008/01/supreme-court-on-copyrightability-of.html),
  [Law Times Journal](https://lawtimesjournal.in/eastern-book-company-and-ors-vs-d-b-modak-and-ors/)). Isliye: sirf
  tarjume ke alfaaz lo, koi note/nishaan/heading nahi; aur 7 aayat ko kisi purani (pre-1935) chhapai ke scan se akshar-akshar
  milao. Online copy (fawazahmed0/dailyayat) me chhapai ki galti mumkin.
- **Digital copy ki shart:** Tanzil/alquran.cloud ki Urdu files "non-commercial" shart ke saath hain — PD text ho to bhi
  unki file uthana unki shart todna ho sakta hai. Hum unhe sirf milaan ke liye dekhein, text khud type karein.
- **Firqa-rang (channel RULES 3):** Mahmud Hasan aur Thanvi Deobandi buzurg; Ahmad Raza Khan Barelvi maslak ke bani. Kisi
  bhi naam se kuch darshak naraaz ho sakte hain. Video me sirf "tarjuma: Shaikh-ul-Hind Mahmud Hasan" — koi maslak-tareef
  ya tulna nahi. Firqa-rang sabse kam: P1 (Shah Abdul Qadir ki buniyad). Wahiduddin Khan (A1) ke vichaaron par bhi kuch
  ulama ko aitraaz hai — halka khatra.
- **"Hindi tarjuma" ka daava:** P1 asal me Urdu tarjuma hai; screen par likhna "Urdu tarjuma (Shaikh-ul-Hind) — Devanagari
  me", "Hindi tarjuma" nahi.
- **Lipyantaran ≠ naya tarjuma:** ek bhi shabd badla/joda nahi (izafat "-ए-" sirf padhai ke liye, ek niyam poori series me).
- **US kanoon (P1):** 1934 pehli chhapai + URAA wala tark anumaan hai; pakka US-PD chahiye to P3/P2 (1908/1911).
- **A1 ka "inform us" shart:** commercial ijaazat ke saath unki maang hai ki unhe publication ka link bheja jaaye; user ka
  niyam "kisi se ijaazat nahi maangni" — ye ijaazat nahi, sirf soochna hai; phir bhi user faisla kare.
