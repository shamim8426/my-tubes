## Ek line
Pehle ki ummaton ke teen aadmi gufa me ruke, chattan ne munh band kiya; har ek ne apna ek sachcha neik amal wasila bana kar dua ki aur chattan thodi-thodi khisakti hui poori hat gayi.

## Hawale (references)
- Sahih Bukhari: 2272 (Kitab al-Ijara; also 2215 Buyu, 2333 Muzara'a, 3465 Anbiya, 5974 Adab) — sunnah.com-style numbering; other editions differ (Darussalam Vol.3 Ch.37 "Hiring" no. 474 per ahadith.co.uk; alim.org 3/37/2273).
- Sahih Muslim: 2743 (Kitab al-Dhikr wa'l-Du'a wa'l-Tawba; alim.org Book 48 no. 6950-6951). One blog shows "2473" = almost surely typo; use 2743.
- Narrator: Abdullah ibn Umar, from the Prophet ﷺ. Chain (Bukhari 2272): Abu al-Yaman <- Shu'ayb <- al-Zuhri <- Salim ibn Abdullah <- Ibn Umar.
- Grade: Muttafaqun alayh (Bukhari + Muslim), sahih.

## Safe / hedge / drop
- SAFE: rain (3465 version), cave, rock from mountain, 3 duas, gradual moving (little, more, fully), milk bowl till dawn, wage grown to cows + shepherd, "don't mock me".
- HEDGE: dinar amount; rice vs millet; "saw the sky" after dua 1 (only some versions, unverified: drop); wife/children crying (optional).
- Man 2, gentle true wording: "meri ek chachi-zaad bahan thi jise main bahut chahta tha. Main ek bahut bade gunah ke qareeb pahunch gaya. Usne kaha: Allah se daro. Main ruk gaya, aur jo diya tha wo bhi chhod diya." No detail of the act, no price. Image 4 "gold_turn_away": only a bag of coins + door, NO woman.
- DROP: names, any "Bani Israil" claim, rulings, "tawassul by persons".
- Introducing them: text says only "those before you" (min-man kana qablakum; Abu Ya'la narration "fiman salaf min al-nas"). Bani Israil is NOT in the text (only a website tag). Say: "Rasoolullah ﷺ ne pichhli qaumon ke teen aadmiyon ka waqia bataya."
