## Visual notes (sirf jagah aur cheezein; koi Nabi/Sahabi/farishta nahi; nau-jawan sirf peeth/silhouette, channel 2a ke mutabiq)
- Pahaadi, sookha-pathreela ilaqa; gufa ka munh uttar ki taraf (IK), andar khuli jagah, roshni andar aati par sone walon tak nahi pahunchti.
- Purana pathar ka shehar: deewarein, bazaar, bhatti/roti ki dukaan; idols sirf door dhundhle (no worship close-up).
- Chaandi ke purane ghise sikke (koi likhawat/chehra saaf nahi). Kutta dehleez par agle pair phailaaye, rakhwali ki mudra.
- Raat ke taare gufa ke upar; subah ki roshni (dawn) pahaad par.
