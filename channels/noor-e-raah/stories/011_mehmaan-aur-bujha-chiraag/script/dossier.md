# Dossier: 011 मेहमान और बुझा हुआ चिराग़ (noor-daily-history, 2026-11-17)

Topic: an Ansari man and his wife host the Prophet's ﷺ hungry guest with only their children's food; they put out the lamp,
pretend to eat, sleep hungry; next morning the Prophet ﷺ tells them Allah was pleased; Quran 59:9 revealed.
Period: early Madinah (after the Hijrah). Exact year: not given in the reports (not stated in the video).

Hadith texts checked 2026-11-17 against the open hadith dataset (fawazahmed0/hadith-api, sunnah.com numbering; sunnah.com
itself returned HTTP 403 to the script). Arabic of 59:9 = Tanzil quran-uthmani.txt bytes (copy in
`.claude/worktrees/hi-30f55e/channels/noor-e-raah/stories/006_imaan-shorts/research/quran-uthmani.txt`).

## Facts (each 2+ sources, all sahih)
- F1 A man came to the Prophet ﷺ; (in 4889 he said he was hard pressed by hunger/fatigue). Bukhari 3798, Bukhari 4889, Muslim 2054a.
- F2 The Prophet ﷺ sent to his wives; they said they had nothing but water (2054a: each one, by oath). Bukhari 3798, Muslim 2054a; 4889 "found nothing".
- F3 He asked who would host this guest tonight (4889/2054a: "Allah will have mercy on him"). Bukhari 3798, 4889, Muslim 2054a.
- F4 A man of the Ansar said "I" and took him home. Bukhari 3798, 4889, Muslim 2054a.
- F5 In one narration of Muslim his name is Abu Talha. Muslim 2054c ("a person from the Ansar who was called Abu Talha"). Only one narration names him -> narration says "सहीह मुस्लिम की एक रिवायत में". Wife NOT named in the video (Umm Sulaym is a later identification, not in these texts).
- F6 He told his wife: honour the guest of the Messenger of Allah. Bukhari 3798, 4889.
- F7 She said: nothing except the children's food. Bukhari 3798, 4889, Muslim 2054a.
- F8 Plan: prepare the food, light the lamp, put the children to sleep when they ask for supper (2054a: distract them with something). Bukhari 3798, Muslim 2054a, 2054b.
- F9 They sat down; she got up as if to fix the lamp and put it out. Bukhari 3798; Muslim 2054a ("when the guest enters extinguish the lamp").
- F10 Both pretended to eat (2054a: "give him the impression that we are eating"); the guest ate. Bukhari 3798, Muslim 2054a.
- F11 They spent the night hungry. Bukhari 3798 ("went to bed hungry"), Bukhari 4889 ("we shall not take our meals tonight").
- F12 In the morning the Ansari went to the Prophet ﷺ, who said Allah was pleased with / wondered at what the two of them did. Bukhari 3798 ("laughed or wondered"), 4889 ("pleased"), Muslim 2054a ("well pleased"). Video says: "अल्लाह को तुम दोनों का वो काम बहुत पसंद आया" (plain meaning; no attempt to quote exact words).
- F13 Then Allah revealed 59:9 "وَيُؤْثِرُونَ عَلَىٰٓ أَنفُسِهِمْ وَلَوْ كَانَ بِهِمْ خَصَاصَةٌ" ... "وَمَن يُوقَ شُحَّ نَفْسِهِۦ فَأُو۟لَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلْمُفْلِحُونَ". Bukhari 3798, 4889, Muslim 2054b; Quran 59:9.
- F14 Background: many Muslims came from Makkah to Madinah leaving homes (Muhajirin); the Ansar of Madinah took them in and shared the date-palm fruits with them. Quran 59:8-9; Bukhari 2325; Bukhari 3780.
- F15 Meaning of the term "Ansar" = helpers (Arabic ansar = helpers; Quran 9:100 "al-muhajirin wal-ansar"). General knowledge, no claim beyond the word meaning.

## Bhaavarth used on screen (59:9 part), saral bhaavarth, own words
"वो दूसरों को अपने से पहले रखते हैं, चाहे ख़ुद तंगी में हों।" and later "जो अपने दिल की कंजूसी से बच गया, वही कामयाब है।"
Checked against Sahih International ("give [them] preference over themselves, even though they are in privation... whoever is protected from the stinginess of his soul - it is those who will be the successful"), Pickthall, and the hadith English above: same meaning.

## Reconstruction (visual only, NOT stated as fact)
Clay oil lamp, woven mats, mud-brick house, number of children shown (reports say "children", no count), the guest's look.
Narration adds no thoughts, no exact dialogue beyond the reported words.

## Excluded (W#)
- W1 Naming the wife as Umm Sulaym: not in Bukhari 3798/4889/Muslim 2054; excluded.
- W2 Other names given in later books for the host (e.g. Thabit ibn Qays per some commentators, see Fath al-Bari): disputed; excluded, only Muslim 2054c's "Abu Talha" with "एक रिवायत में".
- W3 Number of children, the dish cooked, the guest's name: unknown; not stated.

## Depiction (RULES 2e)
Prophet ﷺ never shown (scenes at his place = empty courtyard/doorway light). Host + wife + guest are Sahaba -> back view / far / hands only, no face.
Children: sleeping, from behind, faces turned away. Ordinary townspeople (establishing shots) may show faces.
