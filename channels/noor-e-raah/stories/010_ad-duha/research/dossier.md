## 5. Script-ready kahani (4:00-5:00 Hindi narration ke liye, sirf ye tathya; ~600-700 shabd)
1. Ye Quran ki 93vi surah "अद-दुहा" hai, 11 chhoti ayat, Makka me utri - nubuwwat ka shuruaati daur [P1, P2]. Nuzool ki mash'hoor fehrist me ye 11vi surah hai, Al-Fajr ke baad aur Ash-Sharh se pehle [P3]. Naam pehle shabd "वज़्ज़ुहा" se [P4].
2. Kahani: kuch raatein aisi guzrin jab Nabi ﷺ beemaar the aur raat ki namaz ke liye nahi uth sake; wahy bhi kuch raaton nahi aayi [N1, N2, N5].
3. Ek aurat ne taana diya - "lagta hai tumhe chhod diya gaya"; Makka ke mushrik kehne lage "Muhammad ko uske Rabb ne chhod diya" [N1, N2, N4 - koi naam nahi]. (Optional: kisi ne fikr se bhi poochha [N3].)
4. Tab ye surah utri - tasalli bankar [P5].
5. Ayah 1-2: din chadhe ki roshni ki qasam, aur raat ki jab wo thehar kar sab par chha jaaye [U1, U2, K1, K2]. Mufassireen likhte hain: jaise din ke baad raat aana naraazgi nahi, waise hi wahy ka rukna bhi naraazgi nahi [K3].
6. Ayah 3: "tumhare Rabb ne na tumhe chhoda, na wo tumse naraaz hua" - ek tafseer ke shabdon me: jab se tumse mohabbat ki, kabhi naraaz nahi hua [U3, K4].
7. Ayah 4: aage ka waqt pichhle se behtar - har aane wala daur behtar, aur aakhirat to duniya se kahin behtar [U4, K5].
8. Ayah 5: Rabb itna dega ki tum raazi ho jaoge - duniya me deen ki kamyabi, aakhirat me shafa'at aur Kausar; Muslim 202 wali hadith: "meri ummat, meri ummat" ... "hum tumhe tumhari ummat ke baare me raazi karenge" [U5, K6].
9. Ayah 6: yateemi ki kahani - walid paidaish se pehle, maa 6 saal par, dada 8 saal par; phir chacha Abu Talib ne paala - Allah ne thikana diya [U6, K7].
10. Ayah 7: raah ki tafseel se anjaan the, Allah ne wahy se raah dikhayi (42:52) - "gumraah" nahi [U7, K8].
11. Ayah 8: tangi thi, Allah ne ghani kiya - Khadija RA ke saath tijarat; aur asli amiri dil ki be-niyaazi (Bukhari 6446) [U8, K9].
12. Ayah 9: isliye yateem ko mat dabao - "yateem ke liye meherbaan baap jaise bano"; yateem ki parwarish karne wala jannat me Nabi ﷺ ke paas, do ungliyon ki tarah (Bukhari 5304) [U9, K10].
13. Ayah 10: maangne wale ko mat jhidko - do, ya narmi se mana karo; aur sawaal poochhne wale ko bhi naram jawab [U10, K11].
14. Ayah 11: Rabb ki ne'mat ka charcha karo - yahi shukr hai; "jo logon ka shukriya nahi karta, wo Allah ka shukr nahi karta" (Abu Dawud 4811) [U11, K12].
