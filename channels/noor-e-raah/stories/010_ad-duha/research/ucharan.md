# Ucharan (Arabic -> Devanagari) — Surah Ad-Duha 93 | Khoji, 2026-11-19

Format (RULES 1c + 2i/2m): har ayah ki ek `UCHARAN` line = wahi Arabic shabd Devanagari me, narrator saade tareeke se padhta hai (tilawat NAHI), screen par Arabic ke neeche. Kalam finalise kare. Tags [U1]-[U11] = ek ayah ek tag (93:1-11). Sab ayat chhoti hain (1-5 shabd), isliye jumla-split ki zaroorat nahi; optional split column neeche hai.

**Sources:** Arabic = Tanzil Uthmani v1.1 bytes (dossier 1.1; local file `stories/010_ad-duha/research/quran-uthmani.txt` lines 6080-6090; sha256 PC par baaki). Latin check = Tanzil `ara-quran-la` (`translit_tanzil`) aur quran411 `ara-quran-la1` (`translit_q411`), dono 2026-11-19 padhe (fact T3).

**Rules (as 003-009):** ق=क़ · ف=फ़ · غ=ग़ · خ=ख़ · ذ/ز/ض=ज़ · ث/ص/س=स · ع = sirf vowel ('ayn/hamza vowelon ke beech अ) · article joins as pronounced · ayah ke ant ka aakhri chhota vowel giraya (waqf; tanween bhi) - lambi "ā" (ىٰ alif-maqsura) waqf me bhi rehti hai · beech ki ayah ke vowels rakhe.

**Points to check:**
(1) 93:1 `وَٱلضُّحَىٰ`: Tanzil "Waaldduha", Q411 "Wad duhaa" -> **वज़्ज़ुहा** (ٱل + ض shadda = sun-letter idgham; channel rule ض = ज़, isliye "वज़्ज़ुहा"; Q411 ka "d" Urdu/English rasm hai. Agar user "वद्दुहा" sunna chahe to sirf ye ek shabd badlo - ab tak ki shaili ज़ hai). ىٰ = lambi "ā", waqf me rehti hai.
(2) 93:2 `وَٱلَّيْلِ إِذَا سَجَىٰ`: Tanzil "Waallayli itha saja", Q411 "Wal laili iza sajaa" -> **वल्लैलि इज़ा सजा** (ل shadda; ذ = ज़; aakhri "ā" rakha).
(3) 93:3 `مَا وَدَّعَكَ رَبُّكَ وَمَا قَلَىٰ`: Tanzil "Ma waddaAAaka rabbuka wama qala", Q411 "Ma wad da'aka rabbuka wa ma qalaa" -> **मा वद्दअका रब्बुका व मा क़ला** (د shadda = "वद्द"; ع vowel = "अ"; ق = क़).
(4) 93:4 `وَلَلْـَٔاخِرَةُ خَيْرٌ لَّكَ مِنَ ٱلْأُولَىٰ`: Tanzil "Walalakhiratu khayrun laka mina aloola", Q411 "Walal-aakhiratu khairul laka minal-oola" -> **व लल आख़िरतु ख़ैरुल लका मिनल ऊला** (`لَلْـَٔا` = "lal-ā" (hamza + madd shakl, tatweel); tanween `رٌ` + `لَّ` shadda-laam = idgham "ख़ैरुल लका" (008 "हिजारतिम मिन" jaisa); `مِنَ ٱلْأُولَىٰ` wasl = "मिनल ऊला").
(5) 93:5 `وَلَسَوْفَ يُعْطِيكَ رَبُّكَ فَتَرْضَىٰٓ`: Tanzil "Walasawfa yuAAteeka rabbuka fatarda", Q411 "Wa la sawfa y'uteeka rabbuka fatarda" -> **व लसौफ़ा युअतीका रब्बुका फ़तरज़ा** ("sawfa" = "सौफ़ा", jaise aam bol; ع sukun = vowel "अ": "युअतीका"; ض = ज़: "फ़तरज़ा"; aakhri `ىٰٓ` par madd ka nishaan - narrator saade tareeke se "ज़ा" lamba, tilawat ka madd nahi).
(6) 93:6 `أَلَمْ يَجِدْكَ يَتِيمًا فَـَٔاوَىٰ`: Tanzil "Alam yajidka yateeman faawa", Q411 "Alam ya jidka yateeman fa aawaa" -> **अलम यजिद्का यतीमन फ़आवा** (beech me tanween "यतीमन" rakha; `فَـَٔاوَىٰ` = "fa-āwā").
(7) 93:7 `وَوَجَدَكَ ضَآلًّا فَهَدَىٰ`: Tanzil "Wawajadaka dallan fahada", Q411 "Wa wa jadaka daal lan fahada" -> **व वजदका ज़ाल्लन फ़हदा** (ض = ज़ + madd + ل shadda: "ज़ाल्लन"; beech ka tanween rakha).
(8) 93:8 `وَوَجَدَكَ عَآئِلًا فَأَغْنَىٰ`: Tanzil "Wawajadaka AAailan faaghna", Q411 "Wa wa jadaka 'aa-ilan fa aghnaa" -> **व वजदका आइलन फ़अग़ना** (`عَآ` = "आ"; `ئِ` = "इ"; غ = ग़).
(9) 93:9 `فَأَمَّا ٱلْيَتِيمَ فَلَا تَقْهَرْ`: Tanzil "Faamma alyateema fala taqhar", Q411 "Fa am mal yateema fala taqhar" -> **फ़अम्मल यतीमा फ़ला तक़हर** (wasl: "अम्मल"; ق = क़; sukun ant).
(10) 93:10 `وَأَمَّا ٱلسَّآئِلَ فَلَا تَنْهَرْ`: Tanzil "Waamma alssaila fala tanhar", Q411 "Wa am mas saa-ila fala tanhar" -> **व अम्मस साइला फ़ला तनहर** (س sun-letter idgham: "अम्मस साइला").
(11) 93:11 `وَأَمَّا بِنِعْمَةِ رَبِّكَ فَحَدِّثْ`: Tanzil "Waamma biniAAmati rabbika fahaddith", Q411 "Wa amma bi ni'mati rabbika fahad dith" -> **व अम्मा बिनिअमति रब्बिका फ़हद्दिस** (ع sukun = "अ": "बिनिअमति"; د shadda; ث = स, ant sukun: "फ़हद्दिस").
(12) 93:7 aur 93:8 ki shuruaat "व वजदका" ek jaisi - ucharan bhi hu-ba-hu ek jaisa rakho; 93:9/10/11 ki "अम्मा" family bhi ek shaili me.
