## Patterns (general — nothing copied). Everything from the first file still holds; new points:

### 1. Nostalgia lane: wider, not slower
- Many more channels now (10+ in this lane alone); the top new long videos are 2–3.4 M, several 0.5–1 M; one
  0.5-M-plus video every few days. A video from 13 Oct reached ~1 M in 8 days; one from 1 Aug ~2.3 M.
- Story shapes inside the lane: the journey itself (train, bus, bullock cart, tempo), rain on the way, mango
  orchards, rooftop nights, a missed train / being left behind at a station, a grandparent challenging the kids in
  an old game (marbles), a village fair. Almost all are mood pieces, light on plot and dialogue.
- Many titles put a borrowed style/studio keyword into the title — we never do this (RULES 12a).
- For us: our edge is a real plot + our own cast. "Left behind at a station" is now a crowded beat (note for the
  idea-bank option "मिट्ठू स्टेशन पर छूट गया!"); "grandparent challenges kids at an old game" also taken — avoid both.

### 2. Shorts (new search, Shorts filter)
- Story Shorts that do well are either (a) one relatable family moment (a grandparent taking away phones, a sibling
  moment, a childhood holiday routine) at 20–60 s, or (b) a nostalgia mood clip (first rain, a lantern) at 30–60 s.
- Moral-story Shorts at the top are mostly retold fables (blocked for us, RULES 12b).
- Hooks: a strong single-moment situation in the first line, emotional emoji in the title, no cliffhanger tease
  needed for mood clips; for our story Shorts the no-reveal cliffhanger (RULES 13) stays.

### 3. Bal Diwas (14 Nov)
- Search results are essays, speeches, drawings, wishes and history clips; there are almost no family STORY videos
  for Bal Diwas. A gap: a children-led mohalla story could stand alone in that search.

### 4. Titles / emotion
- Nostalgia: a memory phrase + "90s" / a year + 1–3 emoji (train, mango, rain, heart); 60–100 characters.
- Rain keeps appearing inside nostalgia titles (fits the carry_over planning note: rain / home cooking / family).

### What this means for Kissago (update)
- The lane rewards: grandparent + childhood memory + a sensory place (village, rain, food). We can enter it through
  Dadi's own memories and her village (कजरीगाँव, our invented village) without copying the journey/station/marble beats.
- Bal Diwas is an open search spot for a story; publish around 12–14 Nov (`earliest_date` in upload.json).
- Relatable family-moment Shorts are strong: a Short built on one funny family moment from the long works even without
  the twist.
