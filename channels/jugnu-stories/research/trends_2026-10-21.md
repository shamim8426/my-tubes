# Trends — 2026-10-21 (full research)

Data: public metadata only (yt-dlp flat listings + per-video metadata for 16 videos; WebSearch for channel
discovery). ~290 videos from 3 big Hindi kids-story channels and 5 YouTube searches (Diwali stories, family/dadi
stories, moral stories for children, Hindi story Shorts). No video, audio or thumbnail downloaded; thumbnails not
viewed this round. Limits: YouTube search mostly returned long videos, so Shorts data is thin; upload dates checked
only for 16 key videos; some per-video lookups failed (no JS runtime) — not bypassed.

## Patterns (general — nothing copied)

### 1. Topics (top 5)
1. **Village / grandparent nostalgia** — trip to Dadi's/Nani's village, summer holidays, rooftop breeze, 90s
   childhood (painted AI style). Newest and fastest-rising: 0.4–2.0 M views within 2–10 weeks (Aug–Oct 2026).
2. **Food in the village / street-food comedy** — a poor seller, a greedy rival, a special dish (5–9 M, 2025–26).
3. **Family drama with rich vs poor relatives** (mostly adult saas-bahu cartoons) — very big, also on Diwali
   (shopping, decorating, cleaning; 1–8 M). Not our audience, but Diwali = decorations/shopping/mohalla sajawat
   works.
4. **Old fables and magic/horror** (Panchatantra animals, witches, snake-mothers, mermaids) — the biggest absolute
   numbers (30–117 M) but mostly 2019–2021 uploads; evergreen, old channels. Off-brand for us and blocked by RULES
   12b (retold fables) and our family tone.
5. **Kids' everyday family moments** (a strict dad, a TV habit, a lazy parent — small comic lessons; 3–8 M).

### 2. Hook types
- An emotional situation stated in the title (a poor seller, a rainy kitchen, a journey) rather than a mystery.
- A question or a strong contrast ("X vs Y") — common in family drama.
- A place + time memory (village, summer, train, rooftop) for the nostalgia cluster.

### 3. Title shape / emotion
- Hindi first, then an English or Hinglish keyword block after "|"; 70–100 characters.
- Nostalgia titles: a warm memory phrase + 1–3 emoji (train, mango, heart).
- Family/Diwali titles: contrast or a festive activity + "Hindi Kahani / Moral Stories" keywords.
- For us: our hook question + one emotion word + one emoji is in line; avoid repeating a fixed keyword block (RULES 12).

### 4. Thumbnails
- Not viewed this round. (From titles: the nostalgia videos lean on warm village light and a train/rooftop; the
  family dramas on contrast between two families.)

### 5. Length vs views
- Nostalgia painted videos: 8–11 min. Food/family dramas: 10–28 min. Old fables: 6–12 min.
- Length does not explain the gaps; topic and timing do (same lesson as STYLE_LEARNINGS.md).
- Our format stays 3:40–4:00 (RULES 15).

### 6. Shorts
- Too little Shorts data in this round (search favoured long videos). Next research: add a Shorts-only search.

### What this means for Kissago
- **Dadi + village + summer/childhood memory is the hottest fresh lane** — and Dadi is our lead. A story set in
  Dadi's maayka (village) fits it directly, with our own mystery/heart and characters (nobody else has Golu ki toli).
- **Diwali (8 Nov 2026)**: festive family activities (cleaning, decorating, rangoli, mohalla sajawat, sweets) are
  what people watch around Diwali; we should do it with our mohalla and kids, without rich-vs-poor drama. Publish
  1–3 Nov.
- Food is a strong pull (as in STYLE_LEARNINGS): a dish or sweets as a story element helps.
- Avoid: horror/witch/snake-mother magic, retold fables, rich-vs-poor revenge (RULES 12b, family tone).
