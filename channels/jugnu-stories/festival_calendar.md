# Festival calendar — Jugnu Stories (Oct 2026 – Oct 2027)

Kalam checks this every time it gives story ideas: if a festival/day is **within the next 3–4 weeks** of the planned
publish date, at least one option is about it. Stories are about the festival's joy and family (lamps, sweets,
kites, rangoli, getting together); never mock any religion or ritual, never state religious facts we are not sure
of, never show a ritual wrongly. Dates checked 2026-10-21 (sources below); moon-dependent dates can shift by a day.
