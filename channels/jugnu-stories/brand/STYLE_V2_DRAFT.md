## Design words (all characters)
- Natural, slightly semi-realistic proportions: head about 1/6 of the body for children, 1/7 for adults; long, slim but
  healthy limbs; NO big-head chibi look.
- Oval faces, small delicate nose, small lips, soft rosy blush; very large dark shining eyes with two white catchlights,
  defined upper lash line; lively open expressions (open-mouth joy, raised brows).
- Warm skin tones; warm golden rim light from the sun or a lamp on cheeks and hair.
- Glossy dark hair with visible separate strands and a highlight band; loose strands framing the face.
- Clean thin linework, smooth soft cel shading with gentle gradients; no pencil or rough watercolour texture.
- Environment: lived-in village homes (mud walls, clay-tile roofs, brown wooden doors, potted plants, bougainvillea,
  swept courtyards, trees, distant palms), cinematic sunrise/sunset or soft rainy light, gentle depth of field.
