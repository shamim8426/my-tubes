Ye video nahi, wahi code hai jo video banane me chalta hai. Robot ke sar, aankhein, mooh, haath aur antenna alag-alag hilte hain, aur sab ek hi time function se chalte hain. Loop 12 second ka hai.

0.0 s / 12.0 s

Kaun sa hissa kab hilta hai

Pura robot (aana)

0 – 1 s: baayen se slide-in, thoda overshoot (bounce) ke saath.

Daayan haath

1 – 3.5 s: upar uthta hai, wag karta hai (sine wave). 5 s par card ki taraf ishara.

Aankhein

Har ~3 second me jhapakti hain: sirf upar-neeche ka size ek pal ke liye chhota hota hai.

Mooh

Jab text type ho raha hai tab khulta-band hota hai, baaki waqt band.

Sar aur antenna

Sar halka nod karta hai, antenna ki gend upar-neeche hoti hai.

Body

Saans jaisa halka phoolna. 9 – 10.5 s par dono haath upar (khushi).

Bubble aur card

Bubble me text akshar-akshar type hota hai. Card bounce se aata hai, progress bar bharta hai aur ✓ pop hota hai.

Upar ki patli patti

Poore 12 s ka progress dikhati hai, jaise Shorts ki progress bar.

Ye kaise chalta hai. Ek function render(t) hai: har part ki position us second ke hisaab se nikalta hai (easing formula se). Video banate waqt Playwright render(0.000), render(0.033)... ke screenshot leta hai, aur ffmpeg unhe jodta hai. Isliye animation voice ke saath exact sync rehti hai. Yahan wahi function browser me loop ho raha hai.
