Professional banane me kya kaam aaya

Asli docs data

Har claim docs ke page se. Jo page me nahi, wo video me nahi. Source naam har scene ke neeche.

Playwright (asli Chrome)

Docs ka asli screenshot aur har animation frame ka render. Scroll aur highlight bilkul wahi dikhte hain jo web par hain.

Ek design system

Ek jaisi grid, ek type scale, 8 px ke multiple me spacing, sirf white, black aur yellow. Isse har scene ek hi family ka lagta hai.

SVG icons

Har tool ka apna chhota icon, line ki ek jaisi motai me. Image banana nahi padta, isliye kharcha ₹0.

Easing aur timing

Cards voice ke saath ek-ek karke aate hain, halka bounce ke saath. Scroll dheere, taaki padhne ka waqt mile.

Robot kam, kaam zyada

Robot ishara aur saar me aata hai, har scene me nahi. Zyada hone par wo content se dhyan hata deta hai.
