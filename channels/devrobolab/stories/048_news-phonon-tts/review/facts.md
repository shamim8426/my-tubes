# Facts 048 (AI news: Phonon TTS). Only source: https://huggingface.co/blog/phonon-tts (Hugging Face blog, credits Gradium). Fetched 2026-11-09.
1. Phonon = small text-to-speech model that runs on your own device, no server/network needed. ("Phonon runs on your own device instead. Your text stays there and the voice works without a network.")
2. Made by Gradium (HF team built the Transformers integration; in Transformers from v5.21).
3. Two sizes, about 65 MB and 75 MB.
4. Together they speak English, French, German, Spanish and Portuguese. Hindi is NOT mentioned.
5. More than 100 built-in voices; own voices via Gradium Studio; voice cloning / design supported.
6. Phonon 31m = Apache 2.0 (English only), free even in a commercial product. Other models CC BY-NC (non-commercial; commercial = pricing by number of users).
7. "downloaded more than 200,000 times in its first two weeks".
8. Browser demo: huggingface.co/spaces/gradium/phonon.
