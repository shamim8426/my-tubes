Create & manage schedules for tasks in Gemini

You can put your workflows on autopilot by adding schedules to your tasks. A schedule acts as the automated trigger for a task, telling Gemini exactly when to step in and execute your instructions. You can set these triggers based on time intervals, like every Monday morning, or based on certain conditions, like when an email arrives in your inbox.

To get started, you can schedule a task conversationally with Gemini in a task thread or in a Live chat. Just describe what you want Gemini to do and when to do it. You can also visit the Scheduled page to create it yourself.

Important: This article covers schedules in tasks, which is different from schedules in chats. Learn how schedules in chats work.

What you need

To assign Gemini tasks, you must:

Be 18 or over.
Sign in to the Gemini app with a personal Google Account. For now, this feature isn’t available if you sign in with a work or school Google Account. Learn how to sign in to Gemini Apps.
Have History on.

Important: For now, tasks in Gemini are:

Available without a Google AI subscription in Australia, Canada, India, Japan, South Korea, and the US.
Available with a Google AI Pro or Ultra subscription everywhere else Gemini Apps are supported, except in the European Economic Area, Nigeria, Switzerland, and the United Kingdom.
Only available in the Gemini mobile app, the Gemini app on Mac, and the Gemini web app at gemini.google.com.
