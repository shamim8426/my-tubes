Manage your tasks & workflows in Gemini Apps

You can now start tasks in the same place you start a chat. Assign Gemini tasks to automate complex workflows and manage schedules for ongoing tasks. Gemini can help handle tasks, using info from sources like Connected Apps, skills, chats, websites you’re signed into, Personal Intelligence, location, and more. Just tell Gemini your specific goals and when you want tasks completed.

For example, you can create tasks for Gemini to help:

Save you time: Gemini can declutter your inbox by summarizing or archiving newsletters, create an urgent to-do list after a vacation, and unsubscribe from email lists.
Save you money: Gemini can monitor and alert you when the prices of flights and hotels drop within your budget.
Deep dive on topics & create files: Gemini can pull research with cited sources and package results into downloadable files, like PDFs, documents, spreadsheets, and presentations.

Tip: Schedules in tasks are different from schedules in chats. Learn how schedules in chats work.

What you need

To assign Gemini tasks, you must:

Be 18 or over.
Sign in to the Gemini app with a personal Google Account. For now, this feature isn’t available if you sign in with a work or school Google Account. Learn how to sign in to Gemini Apps.
Have History on.

Important: For now, tasks in Gemini are:

Available without a Google AI subscription in Australia, Canada, India, Japan, South Korea, and the US.
Available with a Google AI Pro or Ultra subscription everywhere else Gemini Apps are supported, except in the European Economic Area, Nigeria, Switzerland, and the United Kingdom.
Only available in the Gemini mobile app, the Gemini app on Mac, and the Gemini web app at gemini.google.com.

Create a task
On your phone or tablet, open the Gemini mobile app  or go to gemini.google.com.
Above the text box, tap Task.
In the text box, describe the task you want Gemini to manage.
You can tell Gemini when to run the task, like around a specific time or after an event. Learn more about schedules.
To specify an existing skill to use, enter @, then select the skill. Learn more about skills.
You can ask Gemini to generate files, like PDFs, documents, and presentations. Learn about files Gemini can create.
You can also tell Gemini to use a custom connected app. Learn how to connect custom apps.
To change the Gemini model or thinking level, tap the model menu at the top. Learn about models and thinking levels.

Turn off task mode

Important: When task mode is turned off:

Your remote browser data and remote code execution data is deleted.
Your Chrome browsing data is not erased. Learn how to delete your local browser data in Chrome.
Schedules will not run as long as task mode is off or if your device is off.
Your tasks, threads, schedules, or any related files that Gemini updated or created will not be deleted.

To turn off Tasks in Gemini:

On your phone or tablet, open the Gemini mobile app  or go to gemini.google.com.
At the top, tap Menu .
At the bottom, tap Settings   Task settings .
Under “Use task mode to hand off work,” tap Turn off.

Tips:

Turning off task mode does not delete any past history from schedules, tasks, thread, or files that Gemini updated or created.
Your schedules and tasks are paused and not deleted.
Your chats are not deleted but you can manage them anytime in Gemini History.
To turn task mode back on
On your phone or tablet, open the Gemini mobile app  or go to gemini.google.com.
Above the text box, tap Task.
Follow the on-screen instructions.

Limits on tasks you assign to Gemini

Gemini Apps have compute-based usage limits that determine how much you can interact with Gemini tools and features. These usage limits increase with higher Google AI subscriptions. How much of your usage is consumed by tasks you assign to Gemini will depend on how many you assign each day and how complex each task is. If you’re close to your limit, Gemini lets you know. Learn more about limits in Gemini Apps.

You can have up to 15 tasks running at a given time. You'll have to wait for tasks to be completed before making another request. Also, a schedule will not run if there are already 15 tasks running.

How Gemini works with you to keep you & your info safe

Gemini’s ability to handle tasks is experimental. As we continuously work to improve it, your supervision is important to help prevent unintended and potentially harmful actions.
