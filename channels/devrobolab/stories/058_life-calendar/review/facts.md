# 058 Claude in daily life 03 (Google Calendar) facts
F1 Connector = Claude can read your calendar to answer questions about meetings, attendees, availability. Plans Pro/Max/Ultra/Team/Enterprise; Team/Enterprise: Owner enables first. (claude.com/docs/connectors/google/calendar)
F2 Connect: claude.ai > Customize > Connectors > Google Calendar > Connect > sign in to Google, grant permissions; button becomes Disconnect. One time. (same page)
F3 Use: in chat select + (lower left) > Connectors > turn on Google Calendar, then ask. Examples: "What meetings do I have tomorrow?", "Do I have any conflicts next week?", "Who's attending the budget review meeting?". Answer cites the events used, with links. (same page)
F4 Access: only the connected Google account, existing calendar permissions apply. (same page)
F5 CONFLICT between official pages: connector page says read-only ("can't create, modify, or delete events / send invitations"); support.claude.com/en/articles/10166901-use-google-workspace-connectors lists create/update/delete events, respond to invites, with "each action ... requires your explicit approval" by default. Video says: pages differ, read the approval Claude asks before any change. (No claim of either.)
