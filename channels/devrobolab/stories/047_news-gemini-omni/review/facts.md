# Facts: Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash (source: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/introducing-gemini-omni-1-2-flash/ , announced 2026-11-05)
- F1 Clips up to 15 seconds (was 10 in Omni 1.1); length settable in 1-second steps from 3 to 15 s.
- F2 Price cut 30% vs Omni 1.1: $0.07 per second for 720p video.
- F3 Better consistency of character/object/scene first to last frame; reference images, short motion clips (<=3 s), audio cues.
- F4 Conversational editing: change product/background/lighting/weather/aspect ratio in follow-ups without regenerating from scratch.
- F5 Available: Gemini API (GA), Google AI Studio, Google Cloud, Flow, Google Vids (15-second clips, no extra cost with Google account/Workspace), Gemini app 15-sec videos rolling out to Google AI Ultra subscribers.
- F6 Outputs carry an imperceptible SynthID watermark.
- F7 Caution (Google): still improving fast sports action, rapid script changes, complex physics.
