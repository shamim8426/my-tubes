# DevRoboLab daily runs: common rules (used by the 08:00, 14:00 and 20:00 tasks)

Each run: 1 long video + 1 Short made from that long video's most interesting part. HARD BUDGET per run: paid spend at most Rs 15 (rules below). Run order inside a run: long first, then its Short.


TECH AND LIMITS: voice = Gemini TTS via scripts/build_deck.py <story> --tts --paid with env GEMINI_TTS_MODEL=gemini-3.8-flash-tts for ALL scenes of one video (never mix models inside a video). Free key first, then the paid key (the user approved paid TTS). HARD BUDGET for this run: paid spend (voice + any paid image) at most Rs 15 in total; paid images at most Rs 4 and only when a free Cloudflare image (scripts/cf_image.py) is not good enough (abstract/symbolic only, no realistic people, no real logos unless NEWS_WATCH 'Brand visuals' allows it, no text inside images). Google balance floor Rs 50: before each paid step check the ledger (.venv\Scripts\python.exe -X utf8 -c "import sys; sys.path.insert(0,'scripts'); import cost_log; print(cost_log.remaining())"); the user's rule is: if the balance would fall below Rs 50, stop all paid work. So before each paid step require remaining minus the step's estimated cost to stay at or above Rs 50; otherwise make NO paid call, finish what the free key allows and report that the user must recharge. If TTS fails (429, network) retry at most twice later; if a video cannot be completed skip it, never lower quality or length to force it, report why. Never run noor_run.py or touch other channels' files. Render with scripts/html_video.py as the recent stories did; loudness about -14 LUFS, true peak under -1 dBTP; verify with ffprobe and a frame sheet (no cut-off text, nothing empty for more than about 2 s); length checks (Short <= 75 s; long 2:00-6:00). Fact-check every claim against its official page and write review/factcheck.md.

REAL REFERENCE ASSETS (user decision 2026-11-05, apply in the LONG videos, and in Shorts where it fits): use real references legally, never unlicensed material. Allowed: (1) free-licence stock photos/clips (Wikimedia Commons CC0/CC BY, Pexels, Pixabay, US-government public domain): open the asset's own page, read the licence line, note author + licence + URL, credit on screen when CC BY; (2) the company's own press kit / newsroom images and its logo (small, unmodified, only to identify it) ONLY when that page clearly allows media/editorial use; (3) a short Playwright screenshot crop of the company's OWN announcement page with the key sentence highlighted in yellow marker and the source name + URL under it. NOT allowed: photos or screenshots from news sites (TechCrunch, Verge, Getty...), another creator's video/clip/thumbnail, company videos downloaded and re-used (link them in the description instead), anything whose licence is unclear. Every outside asset is logged in shared/assets_license.csv (follow its columns) and listed in the description; each long video ends with a 'SOURCES & LICENCES' card. Show outside photos/clips as a rounded INSET WINDOW (about 70-75% width, never full-bleed) with a small pill label above, a tiny credit line below, a slow push-in, dimmed/blurred behind number cards; every clip under 10 s. If no clearly licensed asset fits an item, use our HTML/SVG graphics or an abstract free Cloudflare image instead; never force an asset.

UPLOAD (ONLY after every check passes): upload.json private (privacy private, api_audited false: NEVER public, never publish_at), Hinglish title <= 70 chars (Shorts end with #shorts, long videos have no #shorts), honest description with sources and the disclaimer for non-Anthropic organisations, no spam tags, Made for kids false. Dry run first, then tools\youtube_upload.py --job <story>\output\upload.json. A thumbnail 403 is expected: thumbnails are made by scripts/make_thumb.py (thumb_16x9.png) and the user sets them in Studio. If a check fails do NOT upload that video.

AFTER EACH UPLOAD: topic_queue.json entry (status uploaded + story; JSON format CRLF, indent 2, ensure_ascii False, verify round trip), a log.md line (URL, length, LUFS, cost, rough spots). AT THE END update research/daily/<date>.md (what was made in the evening, links, costs in Rs, balance left, problems, what the user must do) and sync ONCE: powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools\sync.ps1 -Message "devrobolab daily <date> <run>: <n> videos uploaded private" (use $ErrorActionPreference="Continue"; verify .git\sync.lock is gone). Then, if the PushNotification tool is available, send ONE notification (under 200 characters, Hinglish): how many videos are ready, the first link, and 'Studio me public + thumbnail karo'.

NEVER: make anything public, publish, delete project files (move to _to_delete instead), kill processes, put secrets or study downloads in the repo, change RULES/CLAUDE.md, spend over the caps above, copy another channel's wording/frames/voice, or invent facts. Do not start new study downloads. Time budget about 2 hours; run long steps in the background and poll files. If the app was closed at 20:00 and this runs late, still do the same work.
SHORT FROM THE LONG (user 2026-11-05): the Short is NOT a separate topic. Take the most interesting part of the long video you just made (a surprising result, a before/after, the 'wow' step, the best real asset), make it a 45-75 s Short (cap 75 s) with a strong hook in the first 2 s, show only the teaser part, and end with 'Poora video channel par hai' plus the closing line. Put the long video's URL (from its upload result) first in the Short's description. Different title from the long video. Teaching structure for both: hook (problem or benefit, no jargon) -> everyday analogy -> demo/steps -> one caution -> one-line recap -> closing line. Closing line (line A): 'AI के ऐसे काम के features चाहिए? DevRoboLab को follow करो।' via CTA_VOICE/CTA_SCREEN in templates/ydeck_helpers.py.
